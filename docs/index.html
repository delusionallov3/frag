Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Yes. Frag isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer comes from this project's GitHub releases.

Does Frag need OBS?

Only for auto-record. You can drop any recording into Frag and clip it by hand. For auto-record, turn on OBS's WebSocket server and paste its password into Frag.

Why does Frag ask for a HenrikDev key?

Match data: kills, rounds and the scoreboard. The key is free at api.henrikdev.xyz. Without it you can still record, clip and export.

Does Frag work with an AZERTY keyboard?

Yes. Pick AZERTY in Settings. Letter shortcuts work everywhere, and the symbol ones follow your layout: à fits the timeline, ; jumps to the next kill.

Is Frag free?

Yes. Frag is free, and it updates itself from GitHub.

Frag runs in your tray and keeps your games on your PC

Close the window and Frag keeps watching from the tray, with a red dot while it records. It needs no account and uploads nothing.

  • Clips, notes and settings stay in %APPDATA%\Frag.
  • Your HenrikDev key and OBS password are encrypted by Windows.
  • It reads the Riot client's local status, the one Discord uses, and never touches the game.
